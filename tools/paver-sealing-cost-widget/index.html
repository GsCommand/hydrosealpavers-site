What the estimator uses

  • Concrete/brick pavers: HydroSeal's published starting rate of $1.50 per square foot.
  • Travertine/natural stone: HydroSeal's published starting rate of $1.60 per square foot.
  • Failed sealer removal, specialty treatment, repairs and unusual access require separate review.
  • The estimate is planning guidance, not a binding quote.

FAQ

Publisher questions.

Can I embed the widget for free?

Yes. The widget is free to use on relevant websites and does not require an API key.

Does it track or capture leads?

No. The embedded version does not request personal information. Its calculation happens in the visitor's browser.

Do I have to link back to HydroSeal?

No. The widget includes a small branded source link, but it is marked nofollow and is not intended to manipulate search rankings.

Can I change the pricing inside the script?

Please do not alter HydroSeal's published rates and then present the modified values as HydroSeal pricing. If you need a different calculator, use your own pricing and branding.
