What we service

The same service does not mean the same surface conditions

A full-sun driveway in Jacksonville can wear differently than a shaded patio in Neptune Beach or a pool deck in Nocatee. Irrigation, drainage, traffic, previous coatings, joint condition and surface material all affect preparation and product selection.

HydroSeal evaluates those variables before recommending cleaning, resanding, sealing, resealing or additional restoration.

Service area FAQs

Common questions about HydroSeal coverage

What areas does HydroSeal serve?

HydroSeal serves selected Northeast Florida communities across Jacksonville and Duval County, St. Johns County, Clay County and nearby areas. Coverage depends on current scheduling and project type.

Do you offer paver sealing in Jacksonville?

Yes. HydroSeal serves selected Jacksonville neighborhoods and the Beaches for paver sealing, cleaning, joint-sand restoration, resealing and related surface preparation.

Do you service St. Johns County and Nocatee?

Yes. HydroSeal serves selected St. Johns County communities including Nocatee, Ponte Vedra, Julington Creek, Fruit Cove, Durbin Crossing and nearby neighborhoods.

Do you work in Clay County?

Yes. HydroSeal serves selected Clay County properties in Fleming Island, Orange Park, Oakleaf, Middleburg, Green Cove Springs and surrounding communities.

What surfaces do you service?

HydroSeal works on suitable paver driveways, pool decks, patios, walkways and select travertine or natural-stone surfaces after evaluating condition, moisture, joint stability and any existing coating.
