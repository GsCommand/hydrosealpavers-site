Middleburg FAQ

Questions homeowners ask before sealing.

Why do Middleburg driveways lose joint sand?
Large surfaces, slopes, Florida downpours, irrigation and repeated washing can move material from joints, especially along edges and drainage routes.
Do full-sun pavers need different care?
Open sun can accelerate fading and coating wear. We evaluate actual condition rather than using a fixed maintenance schedule.
Can you seal large driveways?
Yes. Larger driveway projects are common, with scope based on cleaning needs, joint depth, repairs, drainage and existing coatings.
Do you also seal patios and pool decks in Middleburg?
Yes. HydroSeal works on suitable paver patios, walkways and pool decks as well as driveways.
