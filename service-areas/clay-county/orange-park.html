Orange Park FAQ

Questions homeowners ask before sealing.

Can old sealer simply be covered with a new coat?
Not always. If the remaining coating is failing, incompatible or trapping moisture, adding another layer can worsen appearance. The existing system should be evaluated first.
What causes dark joints on Orange Park pavers?
Shade, moisture, organics and irrigation commonly darken joints and surface pores over time. Cleaning should address those conditions before sealing.
Do you work on older paver driveways?
Yes. Older driveways are common projects, but the scope depends on joint condition, prior coatings, repairs and how evenly the surface can be restored.
Do you seal patios and pool decks too?
Yes. HydroSeal works on suitable driveways, patios, walkways and paver pool decks in the Orange Park service area.
