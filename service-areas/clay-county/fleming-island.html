Fleming Island FAQ

Questions homeowners ask before sealing.

Why do Fleming Island pavers get dark in shaded areas?
Mature landscaping can keep pavers damp longer after rain or irrigation. That supports organic growth and darker joint lines, especially near beds, pool enclosures and shaded driveway edges.
Do you replace joint sand before sealing in Fleming Island?
When joint restoration is part of the scope, HydroSeal cleans first and restores low joints with kiln-dried ASTM C144 sand before sealing.
Can you seal pool decks in Fleming Island?
Yes. Suitable paver pool decks are evaluated separately from driveways because moisture, shade and foot traffic affect preparation and cure planning.
What if my pavers were sealed before?
We inspect remaining coating condition and compatibility before another system is applied. Worn but sound coatings may be resealing candidates; failed coatings can require a heavier restoration path.
