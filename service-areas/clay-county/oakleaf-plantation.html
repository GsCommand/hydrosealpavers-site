Oakleaf Plantation FAQ

Questions homeowners ask before sealing.

Do newer Oakleaf pavers really need sealing?
Newer pavers are still exposed to Florida sun, storms, irrigation and vehicle traffic. Maintenance timing should be based on actual color, joint condition and surface wear rather than home age alone.
Why is joint sand disappearing from my Oakleaf driveway?
Storm runoff, irrigation, slopes, repeated washing and daily traffic can lower joint sand over time, especially near edges and drainage paths.
Can HydroSeal seal a newer pool deck?
Yes. Suitable paver pool decks can be cleaned and sealed with a moisture-aware plan based on the current condition and surface use.
How do I know whether I need cleaning or resealing?
We evaluate contamination, coating wear, joint depth and drainage. Some surfaces primarily need cleaning; others need joint restoration and renewed protection.
