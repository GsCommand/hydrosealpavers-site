Built for TrailMark driveways and outdoor living areas

Pavers around newer St. Johns County homes still deal with UV exposure, irrigation, organic growth, joint-sand loss and tire traffic. Our process starts with the condition of the surface—not a one-size-fits-all coating. We clean thoroughly, address visible staining where practical, restore joint sand when needed and apply a breathable professional sealer for a cleaner, more uniform finish.

Driveways: restore color, stabilize joints and make routine cleaning easier. Pool decks and patios: improve appearance while accounting for moisture exposure and frequent foot traffic. Walkways: refresh faded pavers and loose joints around entries and side paths.

HydroSeal's paver sealing process

  1. Evaluate existing sealer, stains, drainage and joint condition.
  2. Deep-clean and prepare the paver surface.
  3. Re-sand joints when needed with kiln-dried ASTM C144 sand.
  4. Apply professional breathable paver sealer with controlled coverage.
  5. Provide cure-time and maintenance guidance before the surface returns to normal use.
