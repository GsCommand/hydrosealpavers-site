TrailMark FAQ

Questions homeowners ask before sealing.

Do newer TrailMark pavers need professional maintenance?
Yes. Newer surfaces are still affected by sun, storms, irrigation and traffic. Timing should be based on actual wear and joint condition rather than age alone.
Can HydroSeal restore joint sand in TrailMark?
When needed, low joints are restored after cleaning with kiln-dried ASTM C144 sand before the sealing step.
Do you seal TrailMark patios and pool areas?
Yes. HydroSeal works on suitable paver driveways, patios, walkways and pool areas throughout the TrailMark service area.
What makes TrailMark paver maintenance different from eastern St. Johns County?
TrailMark often has newer, more open-sun hardscapes with less mature tree canopy, regular irrigation and strong storm runoff. Those conditions can create a different wear pattern from more shaded or coastal parts of St. Johns County.
