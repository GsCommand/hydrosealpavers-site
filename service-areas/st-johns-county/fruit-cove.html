Fruit Cove FAQ

Questions homeowners ask before sealing.

Why do Fruit Cove pavers get dark under tree cover?
Shade, humidity, leaf debris and irrigation can keep pavers damp longer and support organic buildup, especially on patios, pool decks and driveway edges.
Can leaf stains be cleaned before sealing?
Visible organic staining is addressed during preparation where practical. The goal is to avoid trapping contamination beneath fresh sealer.
Do you seal Fruit Cove pool decks?
Yes. Suitable paver pool decks are common projects and receive a moisture-aware preparation plan.
What if only part of the driveway looks worn?
Mixed sun, shade and drainage can create uneven wear. We evaluate the whole surface so the recommendation matches the actual condition across the driveway.
