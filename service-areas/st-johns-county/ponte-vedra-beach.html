Ponte Vedra Beach FAQ

Questions homeowners ask before sealing.

Does coastal air change how pavers should be sealed?
Coastal humidity, salt-laden air, strong UV and wind-driven rain all affect wear and moisture. The preparation plan should account for the actual property exposure.
Do you seal Ponte Vedra Beach pool decks?
Yes. Suitable paver pool decks are common projects, and select travertine surfaces are evaluated separately because stone porosity and finish expectations differ.
Should mildew or organic buildup be sealed over?
No. Organic growth and contamination should be removed before sealing so they are not trapped beneath a fresh system.
Can older coastal pavers be resealed?
Often, but the existing coating and moisture condition must be evaluated first to determine whether standard resealing is appropriate.
