Why Homeowners Call Before the Surface Gets Worse

Most Palencia pavers do not fail all at once. The change usually happens gradually.

At first the surface just looks a little tired. But once those differences spread, restoring a cleaner, more even look usually takes more prep and correction than homeowners expect.

  • Missing sand can allow pavers to loosen and shift
  • Dark staining and organic buildup become harder to remove
  • Older sealer can wear unevenly and leave the surface patchy
  • Faded areas stand out more on larger driveways and entries
  • Waiting longer often means more prep and a higher cost later

Many Palencia homes have enough visible hardscape that small problems show quickly. Taking care of the pavers earlier usually leads to a cleaner, more even result.

Palencia FAQ

Questions homeowners ask before sealing.

Why do shaded Palencia pavers turn dark?
Shade, leaf debris and irrigation can keep pavers damp longer, especially near landscaping and along driveway edges. We inspect the surface and treat organic buildup before sanding and sealing.
Can you re-sand a Palencia driveway before sealing?
Yes. After cleaning, we check the joints and refill low areas with kiln-dried ASTM C144 joint sand as needed before applying sealer.
Do you seal screened pool decks in Palencia?
Yes. We seal suitable concrete paver pool decks and evaluate travertine separately to choose a finish and sealer suited to the stone and its exposure.
How much does paver sealing cost in Palencia?
Most projects start around $1.50 per square foot. The final quote depends on the surface condition, joint sand, access and whether an old coating needs removal.
