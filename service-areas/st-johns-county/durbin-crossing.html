Durbin Crossing FAQ

Questions homeowners ask before sealing.

Do newer Durbin Crossing pavers need sealing?
Newer pavers are still exposed to strong sun, storms, irrigation and traffic. Maintenance timing should be based on actual surface and joint condition.
Why is sand washing out of my driveway joints?
Runoff, irrigation, slopes, washing and traffic can lower joint sand over time, particularly near edges and water paths.
Can you seal Durbin Crossing pool decks?
Yes. Suitable paver pool decks can be cleaned and sealed with a scope adjusted for moisture and use.
What if the driveway still has good color but low joints?
Joint condition and coating condition are separate issues. We evaluate both before recommending the project scope.
