Nocatee FAQ

Questions homeowners ask before sealing.

How often should Nocatee pavers be resealed?
There is no single schedule for every property. Sun, traffic, drainage, irrigation and existing coating condition determine when protection should be renewed.
Do newer Nocatee pavers still need joint restoration?
They can. Storms, irrigation, washing and traffic may lower joint sand even on relatively new installations.
Can you seal Nocatee pool decks?
Yes. Suitable paver pool decks are prepared with moisture, shade, splash-out and cure conditions in mind.
Do you work on driveways and patios too?
Yes. HydroSeal works on suitable paver driveways, patios, walkways and pool decks throughout the Nocatee service area.
