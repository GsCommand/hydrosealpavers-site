Ponte Vedra FAQ

Questions homeowners ask before sealing.

What makes Ponte Vedra paver sealing different?
Large premium hardscapes often combine multiple exposures on one property—open sun, shade, irrigation, pool moisture and previous coatings—so the prep plan must match each surface.
Do you seal pool decks in Ponte Vedra?
Yes. HydroSeal works on suitable paver pool decks and evaluates select travertine surfaces separately from concrete pavers.
Can you restore joint sand on large driveways?
Yes. When joint restoration is part of the scope, low joints are reset after cleaning with kiln-dried ASTM C144 sand before sealing.
What if the driveway has been sealed before?
We evaluate remaining coating condition and compatibility first so the next system is not placed over a failing or unsuitable base.
