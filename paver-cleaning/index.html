Professional paver cleaning is more than pressure washing

Jacksonville pavers collect algae, mildew, dirt, irrigation residue, leaf tannins, tire marks, and other contaminants. The correct cleaning process depends on the paver material, stain type, existing sealer, joint condition, and whether the surface will be resealed afterward. HydroSeal approaches cleaning as surface preparation, not simply as a high-pressure rinse.

How a paver-cleaning project should be evaluated

Identify the surface

Concrete pavers, clay brick, travertine, and natural stone do not respond identically to pressure or chemistry.

Identify the contamination

Organic growth, oil, rust, irrigation staining, efflorescence, and failed sealer are different problems. A cleaner that works on algae does not automatically solve mineral deposits or coating failure.

Inspect the joints

If joint sand is already low, loose, or washing out, aggressive cleaning can make the problem worse. The restoration scope should account for resanding rather than treating the joints as unrelated to cleaning.

Inspect existing sealer

Peeling, whitening, heavy film build, or incompatible coatings may require restoration beyond ordinary cleaning.

Paver cleaning before sealing

Sealer should not trap dirt, active organic growth, or cleaner residue beneath the coating. For a sealing project, cleaning must be followed by thorough rinsing, adequate drying, and a check of joint condition, coating compatibility, and weather.

Can pressure washing damage pavers?

Yes. Excessive pressure, an overly tight spray pattern, or poor technique can remove joint sand, etch softer surfaces, expose aggregate, or damage existing coatings. Professional cleaning is about using enough force and chemistry to clean effectively without treating maximum pressure as the goal.

Paver cleaning and sealing

Many homeowners searching for paver cleaning actually need a larger restoration sequence: inspect, clean, treat applicable stains, restore joint sand, allow the surface to dry, then apply a compatible sealer.
