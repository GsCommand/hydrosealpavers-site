What paver repair means

Paver repair can range from resetting a few loose units to rebuilding a failed base across a large area. HydroSeal focuses on selected small paver repairs and leveling associated with cleaning, restoration and sealing projects. Large structural failures, major drainage reconstruction or full installations may require a dedicated hardscape contractor.

Common repairs we evaluate

  • Sunken pavers creating low spots
  • Loose or rocking pavers
  • Small areas of uneven pavers
  • Individual damaged paver replacement where matching material is available
  • Minor pool-deck or driveway re-leveling
  • Edge areas affected by localized settlement or washout

Why pavers become uneven

Settlement can come from base movement, erosion, poor compaction, drainage, irrigation leaks, edge failure, roots or repeated loading. A repair should address the likely cause where practical rather than simply lifting the surface and ignoring an active problem.

Repair before sealing

Sealer does not level pavers or rebuild the base. When repair is part of a sealing project, the correct order is generally to inspect and complete agreed repairs before final joint-sand restoration and sealer application.

Sunken paver repair

For a small localized depression, pavers may be lifted, the bedding layer corrected and the units reset to improve grade and alignment. The repaired area is then reintegrated with the joint-sand system. If the depression is caused by ongoing drainage or widespread base failure, resetting alone may not be durable.

Loose paver repair

Loose units can result from low joint sand, edge movement, inadequate bedding or local disturbance. Joint-sand replacement can improve interlock, but it cannot compensate for a badly failed base or missing edge restraint.

Pool deck paver repair

Pool decks require extra care around coping, drains, screened enclosures and wet walking areas. Repairs should avoid creating abrupt height differences or trapping water. Sealer selection and traction are separate decisions after repair.

When another contractor is the better fit

Large depressions, widespread base failure, major root heave, significant drainage reconstruction, retaining-wall movement or extensive paver replacement can exceed the scope of a restoration-focused sealing company. In those cases, correcting the underlying hardscape problem comes before sealing.
