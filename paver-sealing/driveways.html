Driveway sealing

Driveways take more abuse than almost any other paver surface.

Vehicle traffic, tire wear, Florida sun, heavy rain, irrigation, oil residue, joint-sand loss, and drainage all affect how a driveway should be prepared. A durable result depends on what happens before the sealer is applied, not simply on how glossy the surface looks on day one.

What sealing does

Restore color, stabilize joints, and make the driveway easier to maintain.

Professional driveway sealing can improve appearance, reduce rapid absorption of common staining, and help stabilize properly prepared joint sand. It does not repair an unstable base, correct drainage by itself, or make pavers stain-proof.

  • Improves faded and weathered appearance
  • Supports joint-sand stability after proper preparation
  • Helps reduce rapid absorption of oil, dirt, and organic residue
  • Makes routine rinsing and maintenance easier
  • Creates a more uniform finished surface

Driveway sealing FAQs

Common questions before sealing a driveway.

How much does driveway paver sealing cost?

Pricing depends on square footage, surface condition, joint stability, staining, access, previous coatings, and preparation requirements. Use the pricing calculator for a planning estimate or request a current project quote.

How long before vehicles can return after sealing?

Vehicle return commonly requires about 48–72 hours, but actual cure timing depends on the sealer, temperature, humidity, shade, surface condition, and weather. Follow the cure guidance provided for the specific project.

Do you restore joint sand on driveway projects?

When joint-sand installation or restoration is needed, it is included in the project scope. The amount of joint work depends on existing joint depth, washout, stability, cleaning requirements, and surface condition.

Can old or failed sealer simply be coated over?

Not always. White haze, peeling, incompatible coatings, trapped moisture, or unstable existing sealer can require additional evaluation or stripping before a new coating is appropriate.

Will sealing remove every oil, rust, or irrigation stain?

No. Professional cleaning and targeted treatment can improve many stains, but complete removal is not guaranteed. Some discoloration can be permanent or require separate restoration work.

What if the driveway has sinking pavers or drainage problems?

Those conditions should be evaluated before sealing. A protective coating does not repair an unstable base or correct drainage problems by itself.
