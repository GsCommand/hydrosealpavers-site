FAQ

Paver sealing questions, answered.

Do you offer free estimates for paver sealing?

Yes. We provide free quotes for paver sealing projects throughout Jacksonville, St. Johns County, and nearby areas.

How much does paver sealing cost?

Pricing depends on square footage, surface condition, joint condition, previous coatings, access, and required preparation. Standard paver sealing starts at $1.50 per square foot.

Do you replace missing joint sand?

Yes. Joint condition is evaluated after cleaning and fresh properly graded sand is installed where needed before sealing.

Can you reseal previously sealed pavers?

Yes. We first evaluate the existing coating to determine whether the surface is ready for resealing or needs additional preparation or stripping.

Do you seal travertine?

Yes. Travertine and other natural-stone surfaces require stone-safe cleaning and a compatible breathable sealing approach.
