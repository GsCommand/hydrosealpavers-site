How to Clean Pavers Without Damaging Them

Clean pavers with the mildest effective chemistry, controlled pressure, wide fan tips, thorough rinsing, and care around joints, edges, coatings, plants, and adjacent surfaces.

Updated July 30, 2026 · HydroSeal field guidance

Inspect before cleaning

Identify the paver material, existing sealer, stains, loose joints, cracked units, drainage, and nearby painted or metal surfaces. Cleaning sealed pavers is different from preparing bare pavers for resealing. Test an inconspicuous area because unknown sealers and natural stone can react badly to strong acids, solvents, or concentrated bleach.

Use chemistry before brute force

Organic growth responds to appropriate solution and dwell time. Rust, fertilizer, oil, efflorescence, and tannins require different treatments. One harsh chemical rarely solves every stain safely. Pre-wet sensitive landscaping, control runoff, follow label dilution, and never mix incompatible chemicals.

Control the pressure

Use a wide fan and keep the nozzle moving. Close-range high pressure can etch pavers, expose aggregate, stripe the surface, damage edges, and blast joint sand out. Surface cleaners improve consistency but still require the correct nozzle, machine flow, pace, and overlap.

Protect the joints

Some sand loss during restoration is expected, especially when joints are already low. Aggressive cleaning that empties stable joints unnecessarily creates more work and can loosen pavers. If the project includes resealing, joints should be evaluated and refilled after the surface is fully clean and dry.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use bleach on pavers?

Diluted sodium hypochlorite can treat organic growth on many concrete pavers, but it is not appropriate for every stain or surface.

Will pressure washing remove sealer?

It can damage weak sealer, but pressure alone does not reliably strip a sound coating.

Why are pavers streaked after cleaning?

Uneven distance, poor overlap, chemical drying, or disturbed sealer can create streaks.
