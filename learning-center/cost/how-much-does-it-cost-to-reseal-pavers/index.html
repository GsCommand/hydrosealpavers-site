Jacksonville Resealing Cost Guide

How Much Does It Cost to Reseal Pavers in Jacksonville?

Resealing can be straightforward maintenance or the front edge of a restoration project. The old coating, cleaning, joint sand, stains, repairs and compatibility determine which one you actually have.

Written by HydroSeal Editorial Team · Field reviewed by HydroSeal’s Trident Master Certified team · Last technically reviewed August 11, 2026

Quick answer

Resealing cost should be based on measured area plus actual preparation and restoration scope. If the old sealer is worn but stable and compatible, the project can remain a normal reseal. If it is peeling, whitening, heavily built up or chemically incompatible, the project may need stripping instead.

8 factors that change paver resealing cost

  1. Total measured area
  2. Surface type and porosity
  3. Existing coating condition
  4. Compatibility of old and new sealer
  5. Cleaning and stain treatment
  6. Joint-sand restoration
  7. Paver repairs or re-leveling
  8. Access, weather and cure planning

What should a resealing estimate include?

  • Measured square footage
  • Existing-coating assessment
  • Cleaning and preparation
  • Joint-sand scope
  • Stain limitations
  • Named sealer and planned coats
  • Repairs listed separately
  • Drying and cure guidance
  • Warranty eligibility and exclusions

When not to simply add another coat

  • Sealer is peeling or lifting
  • Surface is persistently white, milky or cloudy
  • Coating is soft, sticky or heavily built up
  • Product history is unknown
  • Moisture or drainage problems remain active
  • Contamination prevents reliable bonding

Common questions

What changes the cost to reseal pavers?

Measured area, cleaning, sand, stains, repairs, old coating, compatibility, access and product requirements.

Can cloudy or peeling pavers simply be resealed?

Not necessarily. Failed coatings should be diagnosed before applying more product.

Is resealing cheaper than stripping and resealing?

Usually, when the old coating is stable and compatible, because stripping adds substantial restoration labor.
