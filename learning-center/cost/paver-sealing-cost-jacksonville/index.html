Jacksonville Paver Cost Guide

Paver Sealing Cost in Jacksonville, FL

The price to seal pavers depends on much more than square footage. Cleaning, joint sand, old coatings, stains, repairs, access and surface type determine the real scope.

Updated August 7, 2026 by HydroSeal

Direct answer

There is no responsible single Jacksonville price that applies to every paver project. A clean, unsealed driveway with stable joints is a different job from a cloudy previously sealed pool deck that needs chemical stripping. HydroSeal uses measured square footage and inspected scope before final pricing.

What changes paver sealing cost?

  • Square footage: controls material volume and labor.
  • Cleaning condition: heavy organic growth, stains or residue require more preparation.
  • Joint sand: low or contaminated joints may need more removal, drying, replacement and stabilization.
  • Existing sealer: compatible worn sealer may be recoatable; failed coating may require stripping.
  • Repairs: sunken or loose pavers add labor before sealing.
  • Surface type: concrete pavers, clay and travertine require different products and preparation.
  • Access: pool cages, tight yards and difficult equipment access can increase labor.

Clean and seal vs. strip and reseal

This is one of the biggest price differences. Standard cleaning, resanding and sealing assumes the old surface is suitable for preparation and compatible coating. Strip-and-reseal restoration adds chemical removal, dwell time, agitation, recovery, multiple rinses, drying and often more joint work.

Cost per square foot

Per-square-foot pricing can be useful for a preliminary comparison, but homeowners should compare what is actually included. A low number that excludes sand, stain treatment, repairs or failed-coating correction is not comparable to a complete restoration scope.

Driveway, patio and pool deck cost differences

Driveways often have vehicle wear, tire marks and larger square footage. Pool decks add water exposure, traction concerns and screened-enclosure access. Patios can have heavy shade, algae and furniture constraints. Surface use changes preparation and product selection.

How to compare estimates

Ask whether the estimate includes measured area, cleaning, joint-sand work, stain limitations, old-sealer evaluation, named sealer, intended application rate, cure instructions, protection of adjacent surfaces and warranty terms.
