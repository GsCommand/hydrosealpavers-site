How Often Should Pavers Be Resealed in Florida?

Most professionally sealed Florida surfaces should be evaluated by condition and commonly fall around a 3–4 year planning range. Exposure, traffic, irrigation, drainage, chemicals, preparation, and maintenance can shorten or extend that interval.

Published and reviewed by HydroSeal’s Trident Master Certified paver-sealing team · Updated August 1, 2026

Direct answer

HydroSeal commonly uses about three to four years as a practical planning range for professionally sealed Florida pavers, but resealing should be based on actual condition rather than a rigid calendar. High traffic, full sun, irrigation, standing water, pool chemicals, coastal exposure, incompatible old coating, or aggressive cleaning can require earlier evaluation.

How long does paver sealing last?

Useful service life is not the same as how long a surface remains glossy. Color enhancement can fade before protection is fully gone, while sheltered areas may look strong longer than exposed driveway lanes. A condition check should consider water behavior, coating stability, sand retention, wear patterns, and surface appearance together.

Signs it may be time to reseal

  • Uneven color loss or exposed wear lanes
  • Water absorption increasing across previously protected areas
  • Joint sand washing out or becoming unstable
  • Coating thinning without peeling or whitening
  • Stains becoming harder to clean
  • Pool-deck or driveway areas wearing differently from sheltered sections

Signs that recoating may be the wrong next step

  • White or milky sealer
  • Peeling, flaking, soft, or incompatible coating
  • Trapped moisture or persistent efflorescence
  • Excessive old film buildup
  • Drainage or irrigation problems that remain unresolved

These conditions may require diagnosis, cleaning, testing, or stripping rather than another coat.

Frequently asked questions

How often should pavers be resealed in Florida?

Commonly around 3–4 years for a professional system, with condition-based evaluation and earlier inspection for severe exposure.

How long does paver sealing last in Florida?

About 3–4 years is a practical HydroSeal planning range, but preparation, moisture, traffic, UV, irrigation, drainage, chemicals, and maintenance change the result.

Should I reseal every year?

Usually not. Unnecessary annual recoating can create film buildup and moisture problems.
