How Often Should Pavers Be Sealed in Florida?

Most Florida pavers should be inspected around every two years and resealed only when the coating, absorption, joint sand, appearance, and exposure show it is appropriate.

Published and reviewed by HydroSeal’s Trident Master Certified paver-sealing team · Updated July 30, 2026

Quick answer

Inspect Florida pavers about every two years, but do not automatically add another coat. Exposed driveways may need attention sooner, while protected patios may perform longer.

Recommended inspection timing by surface

SurfaceWhen to inspectWhat to check
DrivewayAbout every 2 yearsTraffic lanes, stains, sand loss, faded enhancement
Pool deckEvery 18–24 monthsTraction, chemical wear, water exposure
Covered patioAbout every 2–3 yearsOrganic buildup, absorption, shade-related moisture
TravertineCondition-basedAbsorption, salt exposure, breathability, appearance

7 signs resealing may be appropriate

  1. Water absorbs more quickly.
  2. Color enhancement has faded evenly.
  3. Traffic areas look dull.
  4. Joint sand is loosening.
  5. Stains penetrate more easily.
  6. The surface is clean, dry, stable, and compatible.
  7. The existing coating is worn—not peeling, whitening, soft, or heavily built up.

How Northeast Florida conditions affect timing

Jacksonville driveways often face intense sun, heat, irrigation, and vehicle traffic. Nocatee, Ponte Vedra, and Palm Valley properties may have large pool decks, frequent irrigation, shade, or humidity. Jacksonville Beach and Ponte Vedra Beach may need closer monitoring for salt and repeated wetting.

Common questions

Is resealing every two years mandatory?

No. Two years is a useful inspection interval, not an automatic recoating rule.

Can pavers be resealed every year?

Annual resealing is usually unnecessary and can create excess coating buildup, uneven sheen, slipperiness, or moisture problems.

Do pool decks need resealing more often than driveways?

Sometimes. Pool decks and driveways experience different wear, so each surface should be inspected individually.
