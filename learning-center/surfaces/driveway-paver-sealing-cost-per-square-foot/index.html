Driveway Paver Sealing Cost Per Square Foot

Professional driveway paver sealing in Northeast Florida is often priced by square foot, but the true cost depends on cleaning, old sealer, stains, joint sand, repairs, access, and product.

Updated July 30, 2026 · HydroSeal field guidance

Typical pricing structure

A straightforward clean, sand, and seal project may be quoted as a base square-foot rate. More complex restoration should add separate scope for stripping, efflorescence treatment, oil or rust treatment, repairs, and difficult access. An accurate quote still requires measurements and condition.

What should be included

A complete quote should state the areas included, cleaning process, stain-treatment limitations, sand type and color, product, application method, cure times, furniture or vehicle restrictions, and warranty boundaries. Ask whether sidewalks, porches, coping, walls, or patios are included.

What raises the price

Failed sealer removal is one of the largest cost increases because it requires chemistry, agitation, repeated rinsing, and disposal. Deep oil, rust, fertilizer, paint, silicone, or tire staining may need multiple treatments without guaranteed removal. Low joints and sunken pavers add labor.

How to compare quotes

Compare measured area and process. A low per-foot number may exclude sand, treatment, multiple coats, or necessary old-sealer work. Do not accept a firm price based only on the number of driveway cars because two-car driveways vary widely.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a two-car driveway cost?

It depends on square footage and condition. Many two-car driveways fall around 600 to 800 square feet before sidewalks or porches.

Is stripping separate?

It should be, because removal requires much more labor and material.

Are stains guaranteed to disappear?

No. Treatment improves many stains, but permanent absorption or material damage may remain.
