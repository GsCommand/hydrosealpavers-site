ASTM C144 Sand vs. Polymeric Sand for Paver Sealing

ASTM C144 joint sand and polymeric sand can both fill paver joints, but they stabilize differently and should be chosen based on joint width, drainage, cleaning, sealer, and repair needs.

Updated July 30, 2026 · HydroSeal field guidance

What ASTM C144 sand is

ASTM C144 is a graded masonry sand specification. Kiln-dried material can flow into paver joints and compact well when the gradation is appropriate. When a compatible stabilizing sealer is applied correctly, the upper joint can become firm while remaining repairable.

What polymeric sand is

Polymeric sand contains binders activated by water. Proper installation requires clean dry pavers, correct joint depth, thorough removal from the surface, controlled watering, and dry weather. Poor activation leaves loose sand; overwatering can wash binder away; residue can create haze.

Advantages and tradeoffs

Polymeric sand can provide strong independent joint binding when installed correctly. Kiln-dried C144 sand is easier to sweep, compact, top up, and repair. In a sand-stabilizing sealer system, it avoids some polymeric residue risks. Neither option fixes moving pavers, poor base, or drainage failure.

What matters more than the label

Joint depth, clean sidewalls, compaction, edge restraint, water management, and installation are more important than marketing claims. Very narrow joints, oversized gaps, or joints filled with old hardened material may require special preparation.

Frequently asked questions

Does regular sand wash out?

Loose sand can wash out, but proper depth, compaction, drainage, and stabilization improve performance.

Can polymeric sand be sealed?

Some systems allow it, but manufacturer compatibility and cure requirements must be followed.

Does sand stop weeds?

Stable joints reduce opportunities, but airborne seeds can still grow in debris on top.
