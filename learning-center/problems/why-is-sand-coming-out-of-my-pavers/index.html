Paver Problems

Why Is Sand Coming Out of My Pavers?

Joint sand comes out when joints are too shallow, drainage concentrates water, cleaning is too aggressive, edge restraint fails, or the sand was never compacted and stabilized correctly.

Updated July 30, 2026 · HydroSeal field guidance

What joint sand does

Joint sand helps transfer load between pavers and limits movement. It should fill the joint to the proper level without covering the paver edges. Low or empty joints allow more water flow, weed growth, insect activity, and paver movement.

Why sand disappears

Pressure washing directly into joints is a common cause. Roof runoff, downspouts, pool splash, slopes, and irrigation can also create channels. Fresh sand may settle after installation if it was not compacted. Wide joints, missing edge restraint, and base movement accelerate loss.

Loose sand versus structural movement

If pavers rock, sink, spread, or form large gaps, adding sand alone will not solve the base or edge problem. Stable pavers with shallow joints may only need cleaning, refill, compaction, and compatible stabilization.

How joints should be restored

Remove debris and loose contaminated material, dry the joints, install the correct graded sand, compact to settle it, top up, clean the surface, and stabilize according to the chosen system. Do not leave sand piled above the joint or trapped on paver faces before sealing.

Frequently asked questions

Can I just sweep more sand in?

You can top up stable clean joints, but contaminated, wet, or structurally failing joints need more preparation.

Does sealer hold sand in place?

Some sealers stabilize the upper joint, but they do not repair base movement.

Why is sand in my pool?

Low deck joints, runoff, splash, cleaning, or unstable edge areas may carry sand toward the water.
